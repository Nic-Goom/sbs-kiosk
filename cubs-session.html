H is for

Home

Somewhere safe to live and sleep.

Some young people don't have a safe home. That's where Step by Step come in. We don't just hand someone a key — we help them build a place where they can feel settled, safe, and themselves.

"Step by Step gave me a home I feel comfortable in." — young person we support

Supported Lodgings & The Foyer

A young person moves in with a family who has a spare room — or into our Aldershot Foyer, with staff on hand 24/7. Step by Step Fostering also finds long-term homes for children.

325
young people given a safe home last year
E is for

Essentials

From life skills and cooking to health and wellbeing.

Every young person gets their own room — with heating, internet, and in Aldershot even their own bathroom. Step by Step helps young people build the everyday skills they need to thrive.

"It really helped me feel like I had my own space and could do my own thing." — young person we support

Life Skills & Wellbeing

Cooking, budgeting, health — our staff and hosts work side by side with young people to build the everyday skills that most of us take for granted.

79%
of young people we support make positive progress
R is for

Relationships

People in your corner — at every step.

At Step by Step, relationships are at the heart of everything. Hosts open their homes and become a trusted part of a young person's life. Support Workers are there day-to-day. And for anyone who needs a listening ear — from the young person to the wider community — Launch and our counsellors are free and confidential for anyone aged 11–25.

"Actually having people who care about me has helped with my motivation. I think I lost myself for a long time." — young person we support

Hosts · Support Workers · Launch

Whether it's a host family sharing their home, a support worker helping plan the next step, or a counsellor offering free therapy — no young person at Step by Step walks alone.

3,216
counselling sessions delivered last year — a 101% increase
O is for

Opportunities

Chances to learn, grow, and look forward.

A job interview. A college place. A driving lesson. The first weeks in a new flat. Step by Step help young people see what's possible — and take the steps to make it real.

"Step by Step has taught me that my past doesn't have to affect my future." — young person we support

Education, Work & Opportunities Fund

From CV help to college applications — and small grants that make the difference. Bus passes, bikes, a deposit, work boots. Whatever a young person needs to take their next step.

149
young people supported with an Opportunities Fund grant last year

Stage 1 — Go Shopping

The £50 Shop Challenge

How it works

  1. You're a young person living on your own. You've got £50 for the whole week.
  2. There is a 'shop' at the back of the room.
  3. Run to the shop, pick what you need, and bring back the card.
  4. Watch out — your leader might shout out a chaos event!
When everyone has shopped, we'll talk — then move to Stage 2 to write it down.
