H is for

Home

Somewhere safe to live and sleep.

Some young people don't have a safe home. That's where Step by Step come in. We don't just hand someone a key — we help them build a place where they can feel settled, safe, and themselves.

"Step by Step gave me a home I feel comfortable in." — young person we support

Supported Lodgings

A young person moves in with a family who has a spare room. The host gives them a home, mentoring, and the everyday stuff most of us take for granted.

325
young people given a safe home last year

The Foyer & Fostering

Our Aldershot Foyer is a building of small flats with staff on hand 24/7. Step by Step Fostering finds long-term homes for children of any age.

E is for

Essentials

The everyday things you need to stay well.

Food. Toiletries. Clothes for school. A bus fare. A heating bill that gets paid. When you're trying to live on your own at 17 or 18, the basics can quickly become impossible.

"The funding helped me look for rooms more efficiently — I had the option before some others." — Bradley, Opportunities Fund

Opportunities Fund

Small grants for big differences. Bus passes, bikes, a deposit, work boots — whatever a young person needs to take their next step.

149
young people supported with an Opportunities Fund grant last year

Practical Support

Cooking, budgeting, food shops — Step by Step staff and hosts work side-by-side with young people to build the everyday skills.

R is for

Relationships

People who care about you and have your back.

Sometimes you just need someone to listen. Step by Step's Launch service is free and confidential, for anyone aged 11–25 — housing worries, mental health, school, family, anything.

"Actually having people who care about me has helped with my motivation. I think I lost myself for a long time." — young person we support

Launch & Counselling

Free advice and one-to-one support. And free counselling — usually 5 to 8 sessions with a real therapist, in person or online.

3,216
counselling sessions delivered last year — a 101% increase

Support Workers

Every young person we work with has someone in their corner — checking in, helping plan, and standing with them when things get hard.

O is for

Opportunities

Chances to learn, grow, and look forward.

A job interview. A college place. A driving lesson. The first weeks in a new flat. Step by Step help young people see what's possible — and then take the steps to make it real.

"Step by Step has taught me that my past doesn't have to affect my future. Anything I put my mind to, I can achieve." — young person we support

Education & Work

From CV help to college applications to apprenticeships — we work with young people to find the next thing that fits.

76%
of young people in Supported Lodgings made positive progress last year

The £50 Shop Challenge

How it works

  1. You're a young person living on your own. You've got £50 for the whole week.
  2. There is a 'shop' at the back of the room.
  3. Run to the shop, pick what you need, bring back the card and pay.
  4. Write what you bought and what it cost in your StandUp magazine.
  5. Watch out — your leader might shout out a chaos event!
When you've finished, we'll talk about what was hardest to choose, and what you'd keep no matter what.
